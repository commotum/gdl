# 4-ESTIMATE

## Result

- Context estimates use wall-clock net queue burn, while displayed velocity is
  gross durable resolution throughput.
- Estimates require at least ten minutes, twenty outcomes, and positive net
  burn. Growing/flat, blocked, and young samples show explanatory qualifiers
  instead of a date.
- Confidence rises with sample duration and multi-day output is rounded to
  days. The label always says `known queue`.
- Deterministic tests cover shrinking, growing, young, blocked, and zero-ETA
  behavior. Modern history deliberately has no invented global ETA.
